Construction Management: The Art of Building

Quality Work. Quality Results.

  

MCC is a full-service General Contractor and Project Management Company serving the Greater Seattle area. We specialize in commercial  shell and tenant improvement construction projects, including veterinary  clinics and hospitals.


Our team assists and guides our clients in  creating the work place they desire. Whether we’re constructing a  project from the ground up, or completing a tenant improvement, you can  have confidence in us to get the job done and to do it well while we’re  at it.

Our Values

 We work closely with various trades and subcontractors and with our own field personnel when work is self-performed. After each project, we want our clients to feel  they have received quality results from quality work. 


We strive to  exceed expectations every step of the way. That’s what we expect when we Can hire a contractor, and that’s what we provide our clients.
